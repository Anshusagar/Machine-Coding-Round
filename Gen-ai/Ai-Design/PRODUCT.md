# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Singles looking for meaningful connections and highly interactive dating experiences. They value shared interests over superficial matching and prefer to engage in conversation before committing financially.

## Product Purpose
A highly interactive dating platform that connects individuals of different genders based on shared hobbies and deep personality matching. The core goal is to foster meaningful conversations by notifying users and encouraging them to give each person a fair chance to connect. Success means users finding a satisfying match and only paying when they are happy with the outcome.

## Positioning
The only dating platform offering deep personality and shared-hobby matching where chatting is completely free, and users only pay when they successfully find a match they are happy with.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, engaging, and relationship-focused (inspired by BetterHalf.ai). Modern and highly interactive.

## Anti-references
Superficial swipe-heavy apps that gate basic communication behind paywalls or focus entirely on photos over shared interests.

## Design Principles
- Conversation First: Prioritize free and accessible chatting features to encourage interaction over endless browsing.
- Value Before Cost: Clearly convey that the core service is free, and payment is only requested upon successful satisfaction with a match.
- Interest-Driven: Surface shared hobbies and personality traits prominently to kickstart meaningful conversations.
- Fair Opportunity: Use notifications and UI nudges to encourage users to give matches a fair chance to connect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility (WCAG AA), ensuring clear readability, accessible forms for chatting, and support for broad inclusive design principles.
